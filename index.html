Chapter 1 · She arrives

She lands on the store, browses a shawl, and leaves. Nobody knows who she was.

9:40 pm. Meera taps a festive gifting post and lands on Gift & Hampers. She opens the Khadi Semi-Pashmina Shawl at ₹4,853, scrolls the photos, and closes the tab to answer a message. She never logged in, so to the store she is just a visit.
Without fastrrShe is an anonymous session. There is no way to reach her, and the interest ends with the tab.
With fastrrShe is recognised through the ecosystem, and a well-timed WhatsApp message brings her back to the shawl.
fastrr Connect

The ecosystem behind the recognition

fastrr Connect links the store to the Shiprocket network of 1,45,000+ merchants and 42M+ consumers. That network is what lets a returning shopper be recognised without asking her to sign up first.

fastrr Identify

40–50% of anonymous users, identified

Because of the ecosystem, we are able to identify about 40–50% of the anonymous users on the platform. For a store with no sign-in wall, that turns a large share of invisible visits into customers you can talk to, with the right consents in place.

fastrr Engage

WhatsApp that remembers what she looked at

Engage sends the reminder on WhatsApp, tied to the exact product she viewed and carrying her delivery date.

  • Reminder for the shawl she browsed, with the delivery date to her pincode
  • Festive campaigns by category: Gift & Hampers, Men, Women, Wellness
  • Back-in-stock alerts for products that sell out, such as the Desi Ghee
Your strategy manager

Someone who plans the WhatsApp, with you

You get a dedicated strategy manager for WhatsApp marketing and engagement. They plan the festival calendar, build audience segments, write and test message journeys, and review results with your team every cycle, so Engage is run with you and not left as a tool.

fastrr AI Shopping Assistant

A sales manager on the website

It does what a good salesperson does in an offline store, but for every visitor at once, at any hour.

  • Greets and asks. Who is it for, what is the occasion, what is the budget.
  • Shows the right two or three. Not a grid of 500, but the pieces that fit her answer.
  • Answers the questions that stop a sale. Fabric, size, care, delivery, returns.
  • Suggests what goes with it. A shawl with a hamper, a kurta with a jacket.
  • Adds to cart inside the chat. She never has to leave the conversation.
Why it matters for Khadi India

The range is wide, from fabric and grocery to wellness and gifts, and many shoppers are new to buying Khadi online. The assistant makes a ten-category store feel as easy to shop as one counter.

The results it brings
  • 60–70% of customer tickets deflected
  • 80% faster first response
  • 50–60% lower support cost per order
Chapter 3 · She reads the product

The product page answers her three questions before she types them.

10:12 am. Meera opens the shawl. Before she reads the description she wants to know three things: will it get there in time, is there any offer, and what if she is not sure about paying up front.
Question 1 — "When will it arrive?"

Dynamic EDD, pincode already filled

The page shows an estimated delivery date for her pincode, pre-filled from the last delivered address on the fastrr network. She does not type anything. For a gift with a date, that answer arrives before she decides.

  • 96%+ EDD prediction accuracy, from live courier data
  • 12–22% product-page-to-cart lift on brands that show EDD
Question 2 — "Is there an offer?"

A bank offers widget

The offers our team sources show up right on the product page, so a customer sees the saving before she reaches the cart, and not for the first time at payment.

Question 3 — "What if I am not sure?"

A Pay After Delivery widget

A first-time buyer spending ₹4,853 online wants a safety net. The widget tells her she can pay after the parcel is delivered, which takes the biggest hesitation off the page.

Milestone bar

A goal that is always visible

A progress bar shows how close she is to the next reward, such as free shipping or a free gift. You set the milestones, and the bar does the persuading, quietly.

AI upsell

The right add-on, picked for her cart

The suggestion changes with what is in the cart. A gift shawl might bring up honey from the Grocery range, while a kurta might bring up a matching jacket. It looks like advice from the counter, not a banner.

Steal deals

A small price drop on a small add-on

A low-priced item such as a lip balm or towel can be offered at a special price just for adding it now. It is a simple way to raise units per order while she is already buying.

Chapter 5 · She pays

One page. One click. Everything she needs, already filled in.

10:26 am. Meera taps Checkout. The store has never seen her address, but the fastrr network has. This is where most stores lose people, and where fastrr works hardest.
Checkout builder

A checkout that looks like Khadi India

The checkout builder lets your team arrange the page and choose what appears on it: offers, upsell, payment modes and their order. It carries your look and wording, and you can change it without a developer.

One page, one click

No steps, no forms

Address, delivery date, offers and payment sit on a single page, and a recognised customer finishes in one click.

On the checkout page
  • Address pre-fill, 95%+. Recognised customers see their address already filled in, up to 99% for Tier 1.
  • Bank offers. Offers sourced by the fastrr team, shown as their own block.
  • EDD on checkout. The delivery date appears again at the moment of payment.
  • Pay After Delivery. A UPI mandate that charges only once delivery is confirmed.
  • Upsell on checkout. One last relevant add-on, in one tap.
  • Risk-checked COD. If you offer COD, each order is scored for risk before it ships.
QuickPay

Our payment intelligence layer

QuickPay pins each returning customer's preferred payment method as a single tap, and puts the modes she is most likely to complete first. Fewer failed payments, fewer abandoned carts, and a faster second order.

Chapter 6 · She comes back

The first order is a start. Engage and Save and Subscribe make it a habit.

A week later. The shawl arrives on the promised date. Her father likes it, and Meera notices the honey she added. Now the store's job is to be there when she needs it again.
fastrr Engage

Updates, care and the next occasion

Order confirmations, shipping updates and delivery alerts go out on WhatsApp. After delivery, a care message and a review request follow, then a reminder for the next occasion, with your strategy manager tuning timing and content.

Save and Subscribe

Honey, grocery and wellness on repeat

Everyday Khadi products such as honey, grocery items and wellness products are bought again and again. Save and Subscribe lets a customer set a regular delivery at a better price for committing, so the second and third orders arrive without her having to remember.

  • Predictable repeat revenue for consumables
  • A reason to choose your store over a marketplace each month
